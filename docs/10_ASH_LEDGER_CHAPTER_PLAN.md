# Chapter 1 Plan — The Ash Ledger

## Chapter title

**The Falling Hour**

San Francisco, April 1906. The Vectors arrive shortly after dawn while dust, aftershocks, fire, and fragmented authority conceal their insertion.

## Playable party

- **Mara Venn — Operator:** physical intervention and controlled force.
- **Eli Mercer — Ghost:** infiltration, security, and tradecraft.
- **Nia Okafor — Interpreter:** investigation, science, and influence.

Starting HP follows the current Protocol Seven formula derived from CON.

## Vertical-slice scenes

- Impossible publication briefing aboard Aletheia’s field terminal
- Dust-filled South of Market insertion
- Optional rescue of a trapped child
- Insurance Annex search for carbon field sheets
- Telegraph Exchange recovery of Halden’s duplicate request
- Meeting Miriam Quill and identifying her observations as the Carrier
- Silas Venn confrontation: preserve a person or preserve an institution-building record
- Protocol Dice conflict with physical and social victory paths
- Ferry Building extraction
- Historical Verification and appearance of the seven-stroke mark

## Persistent mission variables

- party HP and nanogel supply
- rescued civilians
- carbon sheets recovered
- Halden telegram recovered
- Quill contacted
- Carrier: Fragile, Established, Durable
- Exposure: Clean, Trace, Interest, Noticeable, Severe
- Fire Clock
- Little Giant message delivered
- Venn confrontation resolved

## Developer controls

- `V`: mission-state overlay
- `R`: jump to Venn confrontation after supplying required investigation flags
- `N`: reset deterministic Protocol Dice seed
- `K`: grant all investigation keys for traversal testing

Developer controls must be documented and visibly identified as non-canonical shortcuts.
